Policies & Governance

HOMline DIGI seva operates with full legal transparency, data confidentiality, and customer policy compliance.

Terms & Conditions

1. Introduction

Welcome to HOMline DIGI seva ("HOMline"). By accessing or using our services, website, and support channels (Phone, WhatsApp, Email), you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms & Conditions.

2. Nature of Service

HOMline DIGI seva is an independent digital service assistance platform that guides and assists users with online form submissions, portal registrations, documentation, and digital application processes. HOMline is NOT a government agency or official department.

3. Service Disclaimer & Decision Authority

HOMline provides technical and operational assistance for supported online applications. Final approval, eligibility determination, issuance, timelines, and legal validity of any document or service remain exclusively with the respective official government authority, educational institution, or third-party service provider.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

HOMline DIGI seva collects personal details such as full name, contact number, email address, and uploaded identity/academic documents strictly for fulfilling your requested online service or registration.

2. Document Confidentiality

We treat all customer documents and sensitive information with high confidentiality and standard encryption protocols. Your data is never sold, leased, or distributed to advertising networks.

3. Data Retention

Documents transmitted via WhatsApp or portal uploads are archived only as necessary to track status and provide post-submission confirmation slips.

Refund Policy

1. HOMline Assistance Fee Refund

HOMline service fees cover our administrative guidance, document resizing, and submission work. If a request is cancelled by the customer before processing work has commenced on the official portal, a full refund of our service fee can be issued.

2. Non-Refundable Official Portal Charges

Official government or third-party portal fees (e.g. Passport fee paid to Passport Seva, NSDL PAN charges, Exam Board fees) once paid to the authority are non-refundable per respective government regulations.

Cancellation Policy

1. Requesting Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an active service request at any time prior to portal submission by calling 6282268453 or notifying our team via WhatsApp.

2. Post-Submission Limitations

Once an application has been formally submitted to the respective government portal or university and an official acknowledgment receipt has been generated, the request cannot be cancelled.

Data Protection & Confidentiality

1. Security Standards

HOMline employs secure digital transmission channels and restricted staff access to safeguard sensitive identity credentials (such as Aadhaar, PAN, voter cards, mark sheets, and bank account information).

2. Customer Consent

By submitting files to HOMline via WhatsApp or this website, you grant authorization solely for formatting and submitting your application to the requested portal.
